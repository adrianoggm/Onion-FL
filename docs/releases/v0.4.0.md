# Onion-FL v0.4.0: federated learning techniques

v0.4.0 adds federated learning techniques as plugins, so they can be compared at every level of a hierarchy. Each comparison was run on real data (SWELL-KW and WESAD, three seeds) and is committed under `results/`. The design is in [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-fl-techniques-design.md](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-fl-techniques-design.md).

## What you can do now

- **Personalise** ([#147](https://github.com/adrianoggm/Onion-FL/issues/147), [#152](https://github.com/adrianoggm/Onion-FL/issues/152)).
  - The trainers `ditto`, `apfl`, `fedrep` and `fedbabu`, and the `lg_fedavg` sharing preset.
  - Edges also score their personal and fine-tuned models on held-out rows of their own.
- **Correct non-IID drift** ([#148](https://github.com/adrianoggm/Onion-FL/issues/148)).
  - `scaffold` and `moon`, and the trainer and server optimizer pairs `fednova` and `feddyn`.
  - Algorithms exchange extra state (control variates) as auxiliary arrays, and the server optimizer gets each round's statistics.
- **Defend and attack** ([#149](https://github.com/adrianoggm/Onion-FL/issues/149)).
  - Robust aggregators: `krum`, `multi_krum`, `bulyan`, `geometric_median` and `norm_clip`.
  - An attack axis on a seeded fraction of edges: `label_flip`, `sign_flip`, `gaussian` and `scale`.
  - Central and local differential privacy (`dp_fedavg`, `local_dp`), with ε reported each round.
- **Change the server side** ([#150](https://github.com/adrianoggm/Onion-FL/issues/150)).
  - The optimizers `fedyogi` and `fedadagrad`, next to `fedadam` and `fedavgm`.
  - `fedasync_mix`: FedAsync's mixing rule, applied once per round.
  - A staleness statistic per round.
  - FedBuff-style buffering at the fogs (`close_at_quorum`).
- **Select on validation.** `evaluation.global.subjects: val` scores the global model on the validation subjects, so hyperparameters are never chosen on the test ones.

## Results on real data

All comparisons run over lossless links, so lost rounds do not mix with the technique; `results/techniques_drift_lossy/` studies loss separately. With three seeds, these are findings of these runs, not general claims.

- **Drift.** On WESAD, FedDyn (0.876) and SCAFFOLD (0.828) beat FedAvg (0.752) in macro-F1, and the intervals do not overlap.
- **Server side.** On WESAD, FedAdagrad (0.898), FedYogi (0.875) and FedAdam (0.853) beat FedAvg too.
- **Personalisation.** On the edges, APFL's personal model (0.695) and LG-FedAvg (0.694) are the only ones whose intervals clear FedAvg's (0.641).
- **Robustness.** A strong sign-flip attack collapses FedAvg (WESAD 0.392), and norm clipping is the most stable defence in these runs (0.777).
- **Privacy.** Stronger privacy costs a lot of utility, and local DP costs much more than central DP.
- **SWELL.** On SWELL, most intervals overlap FedAvg's.

The full tables, with `config_id`, `topology_id` and the commit of each run, are in the README (§7) and in each `results/techniques_*/INDEX.md`.
