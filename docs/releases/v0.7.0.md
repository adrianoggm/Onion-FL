# Onion-FL v0.7.0: continual federated learning, the computing continuum and new benchmarks

v0.7.0 closes the continual-learning milestone. `develop` had also advanced well past it: much of what was planned for v0.8.0 to v1.3.0 is already merged and is released here too. The milestones of those versions stay open for the issues they still have.

## Continual federated learning (v0.7.0 milestone)

- **Specification and protocol** for the temporal continuum (#156), migrated from `continuum:` to `continual:` (#193).
- **Model bundles**, checkpoints and lineage between runs (#157).
- **Temporal streams** with delayed labels (#158), edge memory and incremental learning (#159), and unlabelled objectives `pseudo_label` and `consistency` (#160). The selection of the unlabelled objectives is a null result.
- **Continuous federation** with triggers (#161), promotion, rollback and observability (#162), and the Studio, API and metrics of versions with signed operator orders (#203).
- **Benchmark** of continual FL on real data (#163).
- **Dynamic continuum:** host failures and edge reassignment (#190); fidelity harness and `local` backend (#191).
- **Staleness and late updates** (#167), plus protocol, launcher and observability robustness (#175).

## Computing continuum infrastructure (planned for v0.8.0–v0.10.0)

- **Infrastructure core:** specification (#185), core (#186), assignment of the logical to the physical (#187), per-host resources and compute (#188), physical and dynamic network (#189).
- **Backends:** emulated with Docker Compose + netem (#194, #195), gRPC transport (#196), distributed (#199), and distributed continual learning end to end (#164).
- **Fidelity:** profile calibration (#197), simulation against emulation (#198), and the same federation on four architectures (#192).
- **Security:** TLS and authentication on MQTT and gRPC (#227). Operators are opt-in (`ONIONFL_OPERATORS`). An unsigned order counts only when its principal is its sender, so over MQTT it is refused; a signed order is checked by its HMAC on any transport.
- **System heterogeneity:** coupled device–data assignment (#205), FEMNIST with hundreds of clients (#206), system-dependent participation (#207), random per-client compute time (#208), time to target (#209), noise ceiling (#210), a factorised rounds × round-time predictor (#211), and the ex-ante deadline-bias diagnostic with its pilot (#212, #213).
- **Real device traces:** FedScale capacity and availability profiles (#253).

## Learning and observability (planned for v1.1.0–v1.3.0)

- Model families beyond `modular_mlp`, including `feature_transformer` (#229).
- Contribution valuation and explainability (#230).
- Optional Flower adapter (#204) and a comparison with existing frameworks (#202).
- Per-dataset preprocessing for raw pixel and token columns (#246).

## Datasets and benchmarks

- **Stress suite (`onion-bench-stress`):**
  - harmonised Empatica E4 reader and features (#239);
  - datasets: PhysioNet wearable (#240), Nurse Stress (#241) and Stress-Predict (#242);
  - the multi-dataset E4 benchmark (#244) in `results/stress_e4/`.
  - FedBABU and FedDyn beat the centralised logistic regression on Stress-Predict and PhysioNet; nothing separates from the baselines on Nurse.
  - Lab-to-hospital transfer is poor.
- **General FL:**
  - Fed-Heart-Disease from FLamby, cross-silo (#251);
  - CIFAR-10 with IID, Dir(α) and k-class partitions (#247, merged; its CNN reproduction of NIID-Bench waits on #245).
