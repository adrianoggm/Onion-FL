# Onion-FL v0.8.0: fog autonomy, paired statistics and a tested continuum

v0.8.0 closes the milestone of the computing-continuum infrastructure. Most of its infrastructure issues (#185–#192, #205–#213) were already merged and released in v0.7.0; this release adds what came after.

## Fog autonomy (#289)

- **Configuration.** A topology node setting, per level or per node: `autonomy: {after, max_rounds, reconcile: samples|staleness}`. Because it lives in the topology, the `config_id` of every shipped experiment is unchanged.
- **During a cut.** A fog whose parent stops answering keeps closing zone rounds with its children on its latest model, and the samples of those rounds add up.
- **On return.** The fog sends its zone's change rebased on the parent's new model. The parent weighs it by samples, or with its staleness rule.
- **Observability.** The events `fog.autonomous.*` and `fog.reconciled`, `autonomous_rounds` in `summary.json`, `onionfl_autonomous` and `onionfl_autonomous_rounds_total` in Prometheus, and an "Autonomy" row in Grafana.
- **Link outages** in the `emulated` backend: netem at 100 % loss on both ends, recorded as `link.down`/`link.up` events. `sim` already had them.
- Without `autonomy`, behaviour is bit-for-bit unchanged.

## Paired statistics (#288)

- `Runs.compare(..., paired=True, reference=..., margin=...)`, `onion_fl report --reference --margin` and the Studio compare view compare each arm with a reference on the runs that share a seed.
- For each arm they give:
  - Δ with a paired-bootstrap CI;
  - Wilcoxon and exact sign-flip p-values, Holm-adjusted across arms;
  - Cohen's d_z;
  - a TOST equivalence test and a verdict.
- `observability.stats.seeds_needed` computes how many seeds a pilot needs for a given Δ.
- The values are checked against `scipy.stats` and G*Power.

## Models and benchmarks

- **Models.** The `torch_module` model family has four presets (#245). External module classes are imported only from trusted modules.
- **Security of plugin paths (#285).** The config validation imports `pkg.mod:Name` only from trusted modules, and framework paths resolve only to registered plugins.
- **Results.** LEAF CNN on FEMNIST, and the NIID-Bench CNN on CIFAR-10 (#247).

## Configuration

- The shipped experiments have moved from the deprecated `continuum:` section to `continual:` (#309). The `config_id` of all 223 scenarios is unchanged. The scenario names of the two sweeps change (`continuum.trigger,…` → `continual.trigger,…`), and committed runs are matched by `config_id`.

## Test audit (#296–#303)

- **Data and models:**
  - descriptors keep meta columns out of the features, and SWELL legacy parity is strict;
  - trainers are checked on what their updates do.
- **Equivalence and isolation:**
  - real-data equivalence holds across sim and multi-process MQTT;
  - the real runtime isolates node errors.
- **MQTTS:** the client checks the broker's certificate hostname.
- **Operator orders:** edge cases, and `control.unwritten` when the control file cannot be written.
- **Running the suite:**
  - container tests use per-process names;
  - coverage includes subprocesses;
  - the `ONIONFL_TEST_SLACK` timing slack stretches wall-clock bounds;
  - assertions are stricter, and leaked resources are closed.
- **Fixes:** APFL reads `alpha` without its gradient; `pyarrow` is in the dev extra.

## Out of scope

- SWEET (#228) is not used: the experiments use SWELL and WESAD. Its descriptor and tests remain, and they skip without `data/SWEET`.

## Verification

ruff passes. On `develop` at `ba216c1`, with SWELL, WESAD and the other extracts, a local Mosquitto broker and the `onionfl-emulated` image, the suite has 2129 tests: 2126 pass and 3 skip (SWEET).

The CI of this release pull request runs the suite with a broker and without data.
